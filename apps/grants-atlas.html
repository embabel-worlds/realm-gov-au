Five public sources, one answer

What is linked—and what each source contributes

01
GrantConnect published awards ↗

Agency, recipient, program, activity, value, publication date, GA/GO identifiers and the published delivery postcode. The selected inclusive date range is fetched as an XLSX export and cached deployment-wide for six hours.

Live, date-scoped register export
02
GeoNames Australian postcode centroids ↗

Latitude and longitude for Australian postcodes. These points support the postcode-to-division correspondence and the approximate marker positions on the map.

CC BY 4.0 · approximate centroids
03
AEC 2025 federal boundaries ↗

Used at build time to determine which division contains each postcode centroid. A postcode can straddle a boundary, so ambiguous, blank and unmatched delivery locations are counted and excluded rather than guessed.

2025 electoral geography
04
AEC 2025 final House results ↗

Adds division, elected member, holding party and two-party-preferred margin. These are election facts joined to award locations; they do not explain why an award was made.

Official final election results
05
Natural Earth Australia outline ↗

Provides only the recognisable map backdrop. It does not determine a division, a value or any join result; those come from the postcode correspondence and AEC records above.

The virtual traversal

One query joins register, place and election facts

Virtual Cypher materialises the requested GrantConnect window only when the relationship is traversed. An optional ai.relevant stage can judge which awards are about your subject before their delivery postcodes join to the seeded geographic and election reference data.

MATCH (w:GrantWindow {
  key: $from + '|' + $to
})-[:PUBLISHED_GRANT]->(g:GrantAward)
WHERE g.value >= $minimum
  // This line is added only when a subject is supplied:
  AND ai.relevant(g, '<your subject>')
WITH g
// This ordering is likewise added only for a subject:
ORDER BY ai.score(g, '<your subject>') DESC
MATCH (e:Electorate)
WHERE g.deliveryPostcode IN e.postcodes
RETURN e.division, e.member,
       e.heldByAbbrev, e.marginBand,
       collect({
         award: g.gaId,
         recipient: g.recipient,
         activity: g.activity,
         value: g.value
       }) AS grants,
       sum(g.value) AS total
The join is deterministic. No model chooses an electorate or infers a motive. The map marker is the median of a division’s postcode centroids and may be nudged for legibility in dense cities; it is not an official boundary centroid.
Sources: GrantConnect, AEC 2025 results and the GeoNames/AEC centroid correspondence.
Recipient addresses are never substituted for delivery locations.
