Arithmetic before interpretation

Shapes worth opening.

Concentration, repeated extensions, awards near a threshold and June timing. These are structural observations over published records—not findings about motive, value for money or conduct.

Only the selected question runs. Wide date windows are scanned day by day and may continue in the background.
Market structure

Where one supplier dominates a window

Measures top-supplier share by agency and category family, excluding groups with fewer than three contracts. Specialised markets often concentrate legitimately.

Open this when the distribution of suppliers—not an individual contract—is the question.
Change feed

Which end dates moved outward?

Finds amendment events where a contract’s observed end date extends inside the selected change window. One extension can be entirely proper.

This reads the modified feed, not every contract currently in force.
Pattern detector

Awards clustered below $80,000

Surfaces agency–supplier pairs with several awards in the 60–100% threshold band that together exceed it. Routine ordering produces this shape too.

The output is a review list, never an assertion that a requirement was split.
Complete month comparison

June beside the agency’s May baseline

Fetches two complete months day by day. A multiple measures publication timing; annual renewals and budget certainty are legitimate explanations.

This is the slowest check: two full months can take several minutes.
